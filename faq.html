01 Services

What Kriva actually does.

What does Kriva Tech do?

We run five divisions under one roof. Clients usually come in through one and expand into the others:

  • Data collection and annotation. Egocentric video, robotics training data, environment captures, field collection, labeling, and cleaning pipelines. Off-the-shelf datasets or a custom collection program built to your spec.
  • AI and automation. Model evaluation support, workflow automation, internal AI tooling, and consulting on where automation pays off and where it does not.
  • Software engineering. UI/UX design through web apps, mobile apps, enterprise systems, and API integrations.
  • IT and operations. Cloud infrastructure, cost audits, monitoring, and day-to-day operational support.
  • Tech staffing. Vetted engineers placed on your team, contract or full-time.

The common thread is delivery at volume without letting quality slip. That is what "Quality in Quantity, Delivered" means for us.

Do I have to buy more than one service?

No. Plenty of clients only ever work with one division. A single dataset delivery, one cloud cost audit, or one engineer on a six-month contract are all normal starting points.

What kind of companies do you usually work with?

AI and robotics teams that need training data, product companies that need engineering capacity, and operations teams that need their cloud bill and infrastructure brought under control. Sizes range from funded startups to enterprise groups building out offshore capability.

03 Custom Software

Building software with us.

Do you build custom software?

Yes, and it is one of our largest divisions. We cover the full path: UI/UX design, web applications, mobile apps for iOS and Android, enterprise systems, and API integrations into whatever you already run.

Who writes the code? Do you subcontract?

Our own senior team writes it. We do not subcontract delivery to agencies or freelance marketplaces. The people on your kickoff call are the people shipping the work, and the same team owns the outcome through to handover.

Can you take over an existing codebase?

Often, yes. We start with a short technical review of the repository, infrastructure, and open issues, then give you an honest read on whether it is worth continuing or worth rebuilding a specific layer. We will say so if we think a rewrite is a bad idea.

Who owns the code and the data at the end?

You do. IP assignment is written into the agreement before work starts. Repositories, infrastructure accounts, and documentation are handed over in full at close of engagement.

06 Security & Custody

How we handle your data.

How do you handle data security?

Client data lives in access-controlled storage with named access only, and every account is provisioned on a least-privilege basis. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Annotation work happens on managed machines with device controls, not on personal laptops. Access is revoked the day a person rolls off the project.

Every person who touches client data signs an NDA and a data handling agreement before onboarding.

What does chain of custody mean in practice?

For collection work, every asset is logged from the moment it is captured: who captured it, where, on what device, when it was uploaded, who processed it, and who signed off on each QA pass. That log ships with the dataset, so you can trace any single file back to its origin.

Consent and release documentation for participants in video collection is collected and retained the same way and made available to you on request.

Will you sign our NDA and DPA?

Yes. We sign client NDAs and data processing agreements as a matter of course, usually before the scoping call goes into detail. If you have specific compliance requirements, send them over and we will confirm what we can meet.

What happens to our data when the project ends?
